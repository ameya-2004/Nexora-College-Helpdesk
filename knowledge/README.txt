IMPORTANT:
Nexora Institute of Technology and all information in these documents are
fictional/synthetic and intended only for educational demonstration.
Phone numbers and email addresses are sample values and are not real contacts.

RAG behavior:
PDFs -> chunks -> embeddings -> FAISS -> retriever -> LLM -> answer.
If information is not in the retrieved documents, the chatbot should say:
"I don't know based on the uploaded college documents."

Useful demo questions:
- What is the minimum attendance requirement?
- What is the admissions office email?
- What is the tuition fee?
- When does the semester begin?
- What are the library timings during examination preparation?
- What are the hostel return timings?
- What documents are required for admission?
- Who should I contact for fee issues?
- What are the placement eligibility criteria?
- What is the timetable for classes?
- What scholarships are available?
- What is the examination reporting time?
